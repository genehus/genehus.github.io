# GeneHus Clinician Demo — Voiceover Script

**Focus:** clinician preview dashboard only (no problem / product title cards, no status block)  
**Target length:** ~70 seconds · single continuous track  
**Tone:** clear British/Ghanaian-Standard English · steady · clinician-facing  

---

## Script (speak this)

This is the GeneHus clinician preview — a hospital screen for ranking who to see first.

Across the top: GeneHus, the sample hospital ward, and clinician preview.

On the left is **Who to see first** — a ranked sample queue. Highest combined risk sits at the top. Each row shows the man’s name, a short clinical note, and a high, medium, or low badge.

Open the top case — Kwame A. Typical late presentation. On the centre panel you enter age, P-S-A, Gleason, and stage. You can mark family history and switch on a sample African genomic profile — not a real gene file. Update preview refreshes the score.

On the right is **GeneHus Combined Preview**. It shows the risk call, then P-S-A alone versus GeneHus combined side by side, why this preview flagged him, and a clinical suggestion only. The doctor stays in charge.

Sample data only. Not for clinical use. Try it at genehus.bio/demo.

## Timing guide (~70s)

| Time | On screen | Line |
|------|-----------|------|
| 0:00–0:08 | Dashboard opens | Intro + top bar |
| 0:08–0:25 | Queue / Kwame selected | Who to see first + badges |
| 0:25–0:45 | Centre inputs | Age, PSA, Gleason, stage, family, genomic switch |
| 0:45–1:05 | Right preview panel | Combined risk, PSA vs GeneHus, why flagged, doctor decides |
| 1:05–1:10 | Hold / end | Sample-only close + genehus.bio/demo |
