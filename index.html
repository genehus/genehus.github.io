We looked beyond PSA to find the signals that matter

African genomic features and clinical multiparametric models power GeneHus predictions of aggressive prostate cancer risk.

  1. 1

    Hospital-ready clinical inputs

    PSA, Gleason, staging, age, family history, and related clinical signals captured inside existing workflows.

  2. 2

    African genomic profiling

    MADCaP polygenic risk, ancestry-relevant loci, and African allele frequencies not European-ancestry models alone.

  3. 3

    Multidimensional risk engine

    Machine learning brings genomic and clinical signals together into a predictive risk score for aggressive disease.

  4. 4

    Clinician decision support

    Results surface in a hospital dashboard so oncologists can prioritise staging intensity and intervention earlier.

Our current indication focus

GeneHus is concentrated on one beachhead where the clinical need and African data advantage are clearest. Multi-cancer expansion comes after hospital validation not before.

Beachhead indication

Aggressive prostate cancer

Risk stratification for African men combining genomic and clinical signals so clinicians can prioritise intervention before late-stage presentation.

  • Leading lethal male malignancy in Ghana (~2,395 new cases / year)
  • At Korle Bu, more than 80% present with metastatic disease
  • PSA alone has weak specificity for aggressive disease
  • Models trained on MADCaP African cohorts + clinical multiparametrics
Discuss a clinical study

Where we are

Validation underway

Hospital pilots and West African network scale follow successful model validation.

Our diagnostic pipeline

From feasibility to platform a clear path for GeneHus Clinical Intelligence. Contact us to join a clinical study or hospital pilot.

  1. 01

    Feasibility

    Problem framing, Ghana clinical burden, and African genomic data access (MADCaP, local cohorts).

  2. 02

    Model build

    African-trained risk engine integrating clinical inputs with ancestry-relevant genomic features.

  3. 03

    Validation

    Benchmarking against PSA-alone and published Ghana multiparametric models; refining performance.

  4. 04

    Hospital pilot

    Prepare the clinician dashboard for retrospective validation and pilot deployment with target teaching hospitals following ethics and institutional approvals.

  5. 05

    Network scale

    Expand across West African oncology centres with procurement-aligned SaaS pricing.

  6. 06

    Multi-cancer

    Extend the platform beyond prostate cancer once the beachhead is clinically proven.

  7. 07

    Platform

    African precision-oncology infrastructure spanning genomic risk, workflow, and decision support.
